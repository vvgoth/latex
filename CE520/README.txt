HOW TO EDIT
1. Upload the .zip to Overleaf as a new project, OR place .tex and
   metu-logo.png together in the same directory.
2. Set compiler to pdfLaTeX.
3. At the beginning of the .tex file, edit:
   \SupervisorName
   \SeminarDate
   \SeminarTime
   \SeminarRoom
   \SpeakerEmail
4. Edit \ThesisTalkTitle and \ThesisTalkSubtitle if desired.
5. The flume schematic and workflow are native TikZ vector drawings
   in the .tex file, so both are editable without external graphics.

IMPORTANT
- Supervisor, date/time, and contact have placeholders and must be completed.
- CES23 is listed on the course outline; seminar location should be confirmed.
- Wording deliberately treats the ML-based absorption controller as ongoing
  research, not a completed validated outcome.
